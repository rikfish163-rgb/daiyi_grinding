# 系统要求与规格说明（System Specification）

MATHCOUNTS Buzzer System

Equipment Specification

General Description

The system will consist of a central controller and six remote pushbutton boxes which are connected to the controller either wirelessly or with communications cable \(whichever is most cost efficient\)\.  Anywhere from two to six pushbutton boxes will be in use at a given time\.  It shall be used in an academic competition to count down and display the time available for answering a question, and to reliably indicate which contestant answers it first\.  When the first contestant pushes the button on his/her box, it will activate a buzzer sound in the controller and illuminate a light on the contestant’s box\.  All other contestants’ buttons will be deactivated\.  All contestant buttons will be deactivated and a bell will sound if time expires\.  In either case, buttons will be reactivated when the system is reset from the controller\.

Equipment Description

Controller – The controller shall be a light\-weight, portable enclosure with the processing unit and other electronics protected inside\.  The enclosure shall be ventilated as needed to ensure quiet, efficient and safe operation\.  Since the controller is intended to be moved from location to location, components and mountings shall be designed to handle a reasonable level of shock and vibration\.

The sound\-generating component in the controller shall be capable of making three different sounds: two different sounds when a pushbutton is pressed and one sound to indicate time is up\.

On the top of the enclosure, there will be a control that allows an operator to set the time limit to a value in the range of 20 to 60 seconds, a button to start the timer, a button to reset the timer, a small digital display of the time that the operator can see, and a separate button to reset the contestant pushbuttons\.  The front of the enclosure will hold the connection ports for the cables to the buzzers and the cable to the timer display\.  Located on the back of the enclosure will be an on/off button and the connector for power\.

Pushbutton Boxes – The pushbutton boxes will be small enclosures with a button and colored light on top\.  Three of the boxes will have a red light and the other three will have a green light\.  When a button is pressed, the boxes with a red light will make a different sound than the boxes with a green light\.  Each box will be designed to sit flat on a table surface, and to handle sudden, forceful button pressing by the contestants without damage to the unit or injury to the contestant\.  The button should be designed for finger pressing rather than hand slapping\.  The front of the box will hold the cable connector if needed\.

Timer Display – A two\-digit digital display designed to stand vertically so that it will be visible to contestants 25 feet away \(\>= 2\.5 in\)\.

Cables – If cables are used to communicate with the pushbutton boxes and timer display, they shall be LAN type with easy snap\-in connections at each end, and have a length of 25 ft\.  The power cable shall be 6 ft\. long, and can contain a knockdown transformer\.

Description of Operation

Initial Set\-up – Set the desired number of pushbutton boxes on the competitors’ tables and place the controller on a separate table that is visible to the competitors and within 25 ft\.  Connect the pushbutton boxes to the controller, connect the controller to a power source, and turn the controller power switch on\.

Set the timer control to the desired number of seconds\.  The operator should only have to do this once at the start of the competition\.  Press the Timer/Buzzer Reset button to assure all pushbuttons are active\.

At the start of the contestants’ answer period, press the Timer Start button\.

When the first contestant presses his/her pushbutton, the light on that pushbutton box will illuminate and the controller will generate the appropriate sound\.  The system will then record all other button pushes in the order that they are received, before time expires, without any other lights or sounds\.

If the contestant answers incorrectly, and contest rules allow the other contestants to answer, press the Next Answer button\.  This shall not reset the timer, which continues to count down\.  The system will illuminate the light and sound the buzzer of the contestant who was recorded as next pressing his/her button, and deactivate the previous answerer’s buzzer and light\.

Repeat this step as necessary until the correct answer is given or time is expired\.

If a correct answer is given or all allowable contestants have given an answer, and time remains on the timer, press the Timer/Buzzer Reset button\.  The timer will return to the pre\-selected value without a sound and wait to be started for the next question\. Pressing this button will also reset all of the buzzers\.

If time expires before a correct answer is given or all contestants have answered, the controller will make the appropriate sound and all pushbuttons will be deactivated\.  Press the timer reset button to be ready for the next question\.
